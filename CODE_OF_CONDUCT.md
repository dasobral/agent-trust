# Code of conduct

This project is a research laboratory. Contributors and reviewers are expected
to treat each other with respect and to keep security discussions precise.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct, civil, and specific.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Do not overstate cryptographic or authorization claims.
- Do not share private keys, credentials, or exploit details that would harm
  third parties.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or sustained disruption of the
project's work is not acceptable.

## Enforcement

The maintainer listed in [`MAINTAINERS`](MAINTAINERS) may refuse, edit, or
remove contributions and may block people who violate this policy. Reports can
be sent to that maintainer. Vulnerability reports follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md)
instead.
